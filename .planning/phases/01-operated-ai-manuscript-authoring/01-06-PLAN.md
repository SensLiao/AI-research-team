## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| author/integrator -> independent auditors | Conclusions and mutable author state are withheld until independent verdicts are frozen. |
| machine -> vault/run/TeX | Auditors read bounded references and write one run-owned verdict; they cannot promote, compile, or alter canonical source. |
| evidence -> director packet | Structured findings cross into prose without changing severity, disposition, or hash binding. |
